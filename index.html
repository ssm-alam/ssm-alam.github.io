About Me

Research-driven biology

I am a biological sciences researcher with interests spanning computational biology, cancer immunotherapy, molecular drug discovery and bioinformatics.

My work integrates computational modelling, molecular docking, molecular dynamics simulations, genomics and biological experimentation to investigate molecular mechanisms and potential therapeutic targets.

I am particularly interested in translating computational discoveries into experimentally testable biological hypotheses.

Research philosophy

Combining computational prediction with experimental validation to understand biological systems and develop potential therapeutic strategies.
