About Me

Curious about biology, excited by discovery.

I am a biological sciences researcher with research interests spanning computational biology, cancer immunotherapy, molecular drug discovery, genomics and host–pathogen interactions.

My research integrates computational modelling, molecular docking, molecular dynamics simulations, bioinformatics, genomics and biological experimentation to investigate molecular mechanisms and therapeutic possibilities.

I am especially interested in integrating computational predictions with biological experiments that can be tested hypotheses.

Questions That Drive My Research

I enjoy exploring biological questions where computational approaches can reveal new mechanisms, interactions and therapeutic possibilities.

🧬

Cancer Immunotherapy

How can we understand and target immune checkpoint pathways more effectively? My work explores PD-1/PD-L1 biology, cancer vaccines and potential immune checkpoint inhibitors.

💊

Drug Discovery

Can computational approaches help us discover promising therapeutic molecules? I use virtual screening, molecular docking and molecular dynamics to investigate potential candidates.

🦠

Host–Pathogen Biology

How do microbial proteins interact with host molecules and influence infection? I investigate molecular interactions involved in host–pathogen biology.

🔬

Genomics & Bioinformatics

How can genomic data reveal biological patterns that are difficult to see experimentally? I work with sequencing, genomic analysis, SNPs and computational biology.

Research Highlights

A selection of research findings that have particularly interested me throughout my research journey.

RESEARCH HIGHLIGHT 01

PD-L1 Phytochemical Inhibitor Discovery

I explored whether naturally derived compounds could interact with PD-L1 and potentially interfere with the PD-1/PD-L1 immune checkpoint pathway.

Key finding: Hydnocarpin and calomelanol G emerged as promising candidates through virtual screening, docking and molecular dynamics analyses.
Read the publication →
RESEARCH HIGHLIGHT 02

PD-1 Peptide Inhibitor Design

I investigated peptide-based approaches for targeting PD-1 using an integrated computational strategy.

Key finding: Computational screening, structural analysis and molecular simulations identified promising peptide candidates for further investigation.
Read the publication →
RESEARCH HIGHLIGHT 03

PD-L1 Genetic Variations

I explored how non-synonymous variations in the CD274 gene could influence the structure and interaction of PD-L1 with PD-1.

Key finding: Several potentially damaging variants were identified, with C40R and V104L showing notable effects on PD-1 interaction.
Read the publication →
RESEARCH HIGHLIGHT 04

JlpA–Hsp90α Interaction

I investigated the interaction between the Campylobacter jejuni surface protein JlpA and human Hsp90α.

Key finding: The Q332–A354 region of JlpA showed an important role in Hsp90α binding and host-cell adhesion.
Read the publication →
RESEARCH HIGHLIGHT 05

SARS-CoV-2 & ACE2 Interactions

I explored how SARS-CoV-2 spike protein variants interact with human ACE2 and how mutations may influence these molecular interactions.

Key finding: Computational analyses revealed differences in interaction patterns associated with viral variants and mutations.
Read the publication →
RESEARCH HIGHLIGHT 06

EGCG Pharmacological Network

I investigated the molecular targets and pathways associated with epigallocatechin-3-gallate (EGCG) using network pharmacology and computational approaches.

Key finding: HSP90AA1 emerged as an important common molecular target in the computational analysis.
Read the publication →
